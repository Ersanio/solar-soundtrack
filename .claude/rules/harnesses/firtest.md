# `firtest`: what it proves

The echo FIR maths, anchored on the two filters in the SNES manual that AddmusicK ships as
`EchoFilter0`/`EchoFilter1`, and on bsnes's own DSP behaviour. Also the echo-hazard diagnostics
built on it, and that a `$F1` above the first `#N` heads the starting channel's track and pairs with
the `$F5` under it, where one under another channel does not.
